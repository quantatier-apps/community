# Support

Choose the channel that best matches your question so others can find and follow the answer.

## Ask a usage question

Create a post in [Q&A](https://github.com/quantatier-apps/community/discussions/categories/q-a) for installation, configuration, product use, or an outcome you do not understand. If you are unsure whether something is a bug, start there.

Search existing discussions and the relevant [product guide](GETTING_STARTED.md#choose-a-product-guide) first. A useful title names the product, feature, and problem.

## Report a problem or suggest an improvement

Use [Issues](https://github.com/quantatier-apps/community/issues/new/choose) for a specific fault or improvement that can be described and followed up. Search existing issues before opening another report, and add relevant information to an existing report when it matches your case.

For a bug, provide the smallest example that demonstrates the problem. For an improvement, explain what you are trying to accomplish and how the current behavior gets in the way.

## Information to include

Copy the following into your question or report and complete the relevant fields.

```text
Product and feature:
Product version:
Operating system and version:

What I am trying to do:

Steps to reproduce:
1.
2.
3.

Expected result:
Actual result:

Relevant error message or log excerpt:
```

If a workflow uses both Darwin and Cloud, include both versions when available. Say which steps succeeded and where the behavior changed.

## Share a safe example

Remove passwords, API keys, account details, private strategies, and restricted market data from logs, screenshots, and attachments. Use a small synthetic example where it can demonstrate the same behavior.

Write public questions and reports in English. Include enough context in the post for readers to understand the issue without accessing private files.

## Product updates and business inquiries

Follow [Announcements](https://github.com/quantatier-apps/community/discussions/categories/announcements) for official product updates and compatibility notes.

For custom development, integration, or confidential business inquiries, use the contact listed under [Business Inquiries](README.md#business-inquiries).
